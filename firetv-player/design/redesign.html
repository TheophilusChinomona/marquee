Five changes

  • Replace the generic blue with a warm marquee amber; one accent for focus/action.
  • Draw the focus ring over artwork (the old ring sat behind the poster image).
  • Condensed display type and a 15–18sp floor; card labels were 12sp.
  • Add a FEATURED large-poster row as the single hero; keep the Plex rows below.
  • Warm the ground and enlarge margins to 48dp; remove the white focus flash.

Signature

Marquee focus. The amber ring sits on the artwork, the card lifts 3% with a 120 ms settle, and the label turns amber. Reduced motion keeps the ring and drops the scale.
